## Bankroll Rules

- Never bet more than 3-5% of bankroll per pick
- At $65 bankroll → max $2-3 per bet
- Scale up only after 50+ pick sample size

---

## Model Confidence Rules

- Both model AND market pointing same direction → stronger signal
- Model vs market conflict on expansion teams → PASS (thin data)
- Heavy underdog pick under 30% win prob → PASS regardless of edge

## Red Flags — Always Pass

- Expansion team with thin data (under 15 games)
- Model and market more than 30 points apart on same side
- Slight edge (under 5%) on a heavy underdog
- Back to back games with 0 rest days
